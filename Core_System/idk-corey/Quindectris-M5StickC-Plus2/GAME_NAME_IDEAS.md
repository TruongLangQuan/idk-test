# Game Name Ideas - Ultra Challenging Mixed Tetris

## 🎮 **Game Concept:**
- Combination of ThreeTris + Quintris + Tetris + CrossTris
- Mix of 1-4 block pieces for ultra challenging gameplay
- Only the "cool" pieces - not every single variant
- Currently 11 pieces total

---

## 🌟 **Top Name Candidates:**

### **"MorphTris"** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Meaning**: Morphing between different piece sizes (1-4 blocks)
- **Cool Factor**: High - sounds dynamic and transformative
- **Memorable**: Easy to remember and say
- **Fits Theme**: Perfect for mixed piece sizes

### **"VarioTris"** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Meaning**: Variable/Various pieces 
- **Cool Factor**: Professional sounding
- **International**: Works in multiple languages
- **Clear**: Indicates variety

### **"MixTris"** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Meaning**: Mixed pieces from different Tetris variants
- **Cool Factor**: Simple and direct
- **Short**: Easy branding
- **Clear**: Obviously mixed gameplay

### **"FlexTris"** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Meaning**: Flexible piece sizes, adaptable gameplay
- **Cool Factor**: Modern tech vibe
- **Dynamic**: Suggests adaptability
- **Action**: Implies active gameplay

### **"PolyTris"** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Meaning**: Multiple shapes (polyominoes - mono, di, tri, tetra)
- **Technical**: Mathematically accurate
- **Cool**: Sounds sophisticated
- **Comprehensive**: Covers all piece types

## 🏆 **RECOMMENDATION: "MorphTris"**

**Why MorphTris is the winner:**
1. ✅ **Perfect Fit**: Describes the morphing between piece sizes
2. ✅ **Memorable**: Easy to say and remember
3. ✅ **Cool Factor**: Sounds modern and dynamic
4. ✅ **Unique**: Hasn't been used for major Tetris variants
5. ✅ **Brandable**: Works great for marketing/sharing
