────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
🆕 WHAT'S NEW IN v1.2 - DOWN-TO-ROLL MECHANIC!
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The most requested feature is here!

✨ DOWN-TO-ROLL MECHANIC:
  • Press DOWN on joystick while moving → Sonic rolls into ball
  • Release DOWN → Sonic stands back up immediately
  • Perfect control over rolling state
  • Just like the original Sonic games!

🎮 WHY THIS IS AWESOME:
  • Roll down slopes to gain speed
  • Roll through enemies to defeat them
  • Roll to fit through tight spaces
  • Stand up when you need better control
  • Feel like playing the real Sonic!

📝 TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION:
  • Added isKeyPressedDown() to detect joystick down
  • Triggers ACT_ROLL when down pressed and gsp >= 0.5
  • Returns to ACT_NORMAL when down released
  • Maintains momentum during roll/unroll
  • Uses X-axis of joystick (ADC 0) for down detection

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
🎯 COMPLETE CONTROL SCHEME (v1.2)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Joystick Left       → Move Sonic left
Joystick Right      → Move Sonic right
Joystick DOWN       → Roll (press to roll, release to stand) 🆕
Joystick Button     → Jump
BtnA + Joy Button   → Spindash (standing) / Roll (moving)

NOW YOU HAVE 2 WAYS TO ROLL:
  1. Press DOWN (can toggle on/off) 🆕
  2. Hold BtnA + Joy Button (continuous hold)
